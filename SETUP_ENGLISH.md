# Sellora AI — Merchant User Guide & Quickstart

Welcome to **Sellora AI — Smart Sales Assistant for Online Businesses**.

---

## 1. Quickstart (3 Easy Steps)

### Step 1: Complete Store Setup Wizard
- Open the application and run the 4-step wizard.
- Pick your business category (from all 15 niches: Boutique, Jewellery, Saree, Bags, Skincare, etc.).
- Set your local currency (`₹ INR`, `$ USD`, `€ EUR`, etc.) and brand tone.
- Save your delivery timeframes and return rules.

### Step 2: Add Products or Import CSV
- Go to the **Catalog** tab.
- Tap **+** to add your inventory with prices, colors, sizes, and stock status.
- Or tap **Import CSV** to batch-load your entire catalog in seconds.

### Step 3: Generate Instant Grounded AI Sales Replies
- Switch to the **AI Reply** tab.
- Paste your customer's message or tap one of the quick chips.
- Select the matching product from your catalog.
- Choose your reply language: **English**, **Hindi (हिंदी)**, or **Bengali (বাংলা)**.
- Tap **Generate Sales Replies**.
- Copy your favorite variant and paste directly into WhatsApp or Instagram DM!

---

## 2. Managing Customer Inquiries (Leads)
- When a buyer shows purchase intent, tap **Save as Lead** from the generator.
- Track their stage (`Interested`, `Follow-up Needed`, `Converted`).
- Tap **Draft AI Reply** on any lead to generate custom follow-ups.
